## Sampling

We sample our data when we do not have access to all of the data in the real world or when we cannot process all of the data that we have. 

### Non-probability Sampling

Convenience sampling: select data based on availability. As its name suggests, it is popular because it is convenient. 

### Simple Random Sampling

Select the data equally randomly. It is easy to implement, and all members of the population are given the same chance of being selected. However, rare categories may not appear in the sample.
