- **Architecture:** a fine-tuned CLIP visual encoder produces spatio-temporal features, a linear projection maps them into Vicuna-v1.1 (initialised with LLaVA weights), and the text predictions of two classification heads are fed to the LLM alongside the video features.
- **Why it matters for the thesis:** the explanation is generated *conditioned on a decision the classifier already made*, so the LLM may be rationalising rather than reading the video.
- **Experiment it suggests:** swap the injected prediction and check whether the explanation flips to justify it.
