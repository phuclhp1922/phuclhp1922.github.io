- **Idea:** freeze the pretrained weights and learn a low-rank update $$\Delta W = BA$$ for selected weight matrices.
- **Why it works:** the weight change needed for adaptation appears to have low intrinsic rank.
- **Practical upshot:** far fewer trainable parameters, and the update can be merged into $$W_0$$ after training, so inference has no extra latency.
